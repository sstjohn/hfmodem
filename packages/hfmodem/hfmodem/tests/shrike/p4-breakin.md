# P4 changeover and turn recordings

The WAV files in `fixtures/p4-breakin/` are mono signed 16-bit PCM at 12 kHz. Each JSON sidecar records
the source recording, crop coordinates, sample-rate correction, frequency
shift where applicable, and SHA-256 digest. These are receive recordings, not
samples generated by the transmitter being tested.

- `kb5lzk-audio`: greeting from the September 26 after-arm recording. The
  recorded stream lost 512-sample buffers. The sidecar names the reconstruction
  method and the 1,199 inserted buffers; its time coordinates refer to that
  reconstructed timeline. The one-second fixture spans 17–18 seconds there.
- `ve3kpg-audio`: Y27 and the greeting from the September 26 12:28 arm,
  35.94–36.90 seconds in the receive stream.
- `n9vc-idle`, `n9vc-close`, `n9vc-sid`: Y27 and independently decoded
  changeover fields from the September 25 Kiwi recordings. Status bytes are
  respectively 0x40, 0x20 and 0x00.
- `n9vc-x27`, `n9vc-v27`, `n9vc-u27`, `n9vc-t27`: individual answer words.
- `n9vc-normal`: the normal short at source time 1458.160 seconds. Its lattice
  identifies timing; no payload decode is asserted.
- `n9vc-long-ack`: the robust long at 1464.696 seconds and X27 at +3.414.
  The long is weak enough that a 6 dB envelope threshold fragments it. The
  receiver uses a 3 dB envelope with at most 35 ms of interior fade bridging
  only after a coherent head has been found at the expected packet position.

The N9VC crops use the measured source rates around 11998.908 Hz, upsample
fourfold before a −910 Hz analytic frequency shift, then resample to 12 kHz.
Their sidecars retain the per-recording rates and exact crop endpoints.

`kb5lzk.bits` and `ve3kpg.bits` contain 840 recorded coded bits in channel
order and wire polarity, independent of the renderer: re-encoding the
established greetings differs at zero and one bit respectively. The polarity
inversion from the original extraction is described beside the tests, in
`p4-breakin-kb5lzk.json` and `p4-breakin-ve3kpg.json`.
