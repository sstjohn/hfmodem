    Accessibility

    This changes screen-reader announcements. All notices remain in Console events. Radio capture and decoding are unaffected.

    Use Tab to reach controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Frequency, gain and playback controls also accept arrow keys. Escape closes a dialog; outside dialogs it stops a simulated transmission.

    Choose waterfall colors in the Receiver. Browser zoom and your system’s reduced-motion preference are supported.

    Radio terms
    Receive / RX
    Listening for incoming signals.
    Transmit / TX
    Sending a signal.
    Push to talk / PTT
    The radio’s transmit control.
    VFO A
    The radio’s primary frequency setting.
    Signal-to-noise ratio / SNR
    Signal strength compared with background noise, in decibels.
    QSO
    A radio contact or exchange.

    WCAG 2.2 AA is the required baseline. Full conformance has not yet been verified.
