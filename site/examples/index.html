Reading the evidence

A CRC-valid frame establishes reception of that frame. A local transmit recording alone does not show that the peer received it. A peer acknowledgment confirms link reception; a destination receipt confirms mail delivery. Each example states which evidence is available.

The ARDOP examples use remote KiwiSDR recordings. Their original rig recordings and an older high-speed Winlink VARA receive recording were lost. The VarAC recording is a separate session.

Implementation status includes results supported by station logs and operator reports.
