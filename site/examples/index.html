The ARDOP recordings come from remote KiwiSDR witnesses. The WW2MI message reached its destination, but our subsequent receive parser failed: three offered messages were not fetched, and END was not confirmed. W6IDS delivered three messages and closed the link normally.

Original completed-message records establish mail direction and completion. Destination receipts establish delivery where explicitly stated; gateway acceptance and destination delivery are separate evidence. Remote gateways are interoperating stations, not demonstrations of native hfmodem at both ends.

Recordings and packet replay may have different coverage. A local monitor tap shows our audio, not reception at the peer; a remote witness shows what its receiver heard. Sender colors use the verified local transmit schedule. Read the implementation evidence for broader results and limits.
